Work. Connect. Create.

These are the Third Spaces across Cape Town’s Inner City where you’re welcome to work, connect with new people, share ideas, and create.

Third Spaces are more than just locations. They’re shared spaces where young people can connect, collaborate, and build a sense of belonging.

Visit a Third Space, bring a friend, start a conversation, and make place in the Inner City!
